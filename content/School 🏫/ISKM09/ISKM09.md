# Reflexia
Predmet prezentuje prístupy a aktivity akademických kníižníc v zahraničí. Je dobré nevymeškať ani jednu prezentáciu, účasť je zaznamenávaná. K splneniu potrebná seminárna práca s pomerne veľkým rozsahom - je treba si vyhradiť dostatočne veľa času. Nasledujúci feedback bol súčasťou náhrady mojej vymeškanej hodiny:

**A. Content Reflection**
1. Which one or two presentations inspired you the most, and why?
    - Definitely the presentation from **Harrie van der Meer** about the academic library in Holand. I was fascinated by the extent of AI used in their library – the learning materials AI generator sounds like a helpful way of using the AI, it was also nice to hear the progress, how they started with QR codes and iterated to Custom GPT app. It was also good to hear the extent of AI workshops they are providing, and the breakdown of AI research methods in comparison to traditional ways was very helpful.
2. What surprised you about the approaches to academic librarianship in other countries (positively or negatively)?
    - Since I know very little about academic libraries in this country, much of the information was new to me. I was surprised by the complexity of the libraries, the management, by the extent of their activities and the diversity of funding sources – in the University of Toronto, there were many sources and surprising was that the endowments were one of the important ones.
    - I was a bit negatively surprised by the lack of AI focus in other countries besides Netherlands.
3. Did any presentation challenge or shift your understanding of the role of an academic library?

- The presentation about University of Amsterdam library – it has shifted my view of libraries primary target group being students, to both teachers and students and also researchers.

**B. Evaluation & Comparison**
1. Which services, processes, or strategies seemed the most innovative abroad?
    - Custom GPT learning material generator
    - Doctors can prescribe Park Prescriptions
    - Turning libraries into learning hubs
    - Scholarship of Teaching and Learning
2. Which aspects seemed problematic, unrealistic, or unsuitable for Czech academic libraries?
    - I don’t know, I think a lot of their approaches could be applicable here – the only constrain I’d say would be the lack of funding options, less target people and less cooperations with other institutions.
3. In which areas do Czech academic libraries already excel in the context of the examples presented?

- I think, our libraries are also turning to learning hubs, with a comfy beanbags, strategically placed outlets and a pleasant place to study.

**C. Inspiration for the Czech Context**
1. Select 2–3 realistic inspirations that could be implemented in Czech academic libraries. Why these?

- AI research manual containing important checkboxes to ensure the quality of the sources – I lack AI research options in our library and I think many of students already use AI to a big extent, so I think it would be helpful.
- Knowledge hubs in libraries with a place to craft and prototype – with 3D printers, cutters and even a podcast hub.

1. What benefits would these ideas bring to users (students, teachers) and to the institution?

- Quality of research, AI literacy growth, creativity development.

1. What barriers or challenges would need to be overcome to make these inspirations feasible?

- Clear design of AI manual, a way to persuade students to use it, iterations of the manual to ensure the relevance of the information.
- Dedicate a place in library for the knowledge hub.

1. What should be the first steps for Czech libraries that wish to adopt these ideas?

- For the knowledge hub – student survey on their needs and views.
- For AI manual – research to find the lacking factors of AI research options (relevancy, verifiability…)

**D. Course Reflection**
1. If you had to definitely **keep** one thing from the course, what would it be?

- Academic libraries are complex institutions with an important role in research, collaboration, knowledge support and development.

1. If you had to definitely **change** one thing from the course, what would it be?

- Less facts and background, more innovative approaches.
